## Overview
A Tableau-based analysis of ~4,000 Amazon customers' purchasing behavior, combining e-commerce transaction data with U.S. Census population data to uncover demographic, preference, and time-based patterns for business decision-making.

## Objectives
- Understand customer demographics and how they relate to spending
- Identify product, color, and category preferences across customer segments
- Track seasonal and time-based sales trends
- Pinpoint high-value customers and states for targeted strategy

## Methodology
- Built a multi-table data pipeline in Tableau Prep (Flow), joining user profiles, product details, user-product interactions, and U.S. population data
- Cleaned and restructured raw fields (e.g., splitting category hierarchies, converting timestamps, filtering to Amazon-sold products only)
- Built four interactive dashboards: Customer Demographics, Customer Preferences, Time Series Analysis, and High-Value Customers, each with cross-filtering

## Outcomes
Found that spending increases with age for both genders, that Venmo dominates among younger customers while traditional methods persist in older groups, and that specific states (e.g., Montana, North Dakota) combine low population with disproportionately high spending, informing targeted, demographic-specific marketing recommendations.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data pipeline design (Tableau Prep / Flow)
- Data cleaning and restructuring
- Multi-table joins across heterogeneous datasets
- Interactive dashboard design
- Cross-filtering and demographic segmentation
- Business insight synthesis
