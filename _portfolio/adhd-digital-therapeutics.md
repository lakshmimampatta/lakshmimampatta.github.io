**Overview:** Explored how metaverse-based therapeutic environments can engage ADHD adolescents while avoiding sensory overstimulation.
**Objectives:** Understand stakeholder needs; identify personalization strategies for engagement; identify sensory-management strategies to reduce overstimulation.
**Methodology:** Qualitative multiple-case study — 17 semi-structured interviews (therapists, designers, parents, teachers), analyzed using the Gioia Methodology in NVivo.
**Outcomes:** Delivered a preliminary framework that offers concrete guidance for building safer, more engaging therapeutic metaverse platforms for ADHD adolescents.
